Behind the Music: Our Team Prepares for the Season

Every great performance starts long before anyone steps on stage. This week, Justin Koo spent his afternoon unboxing and organizing a fresh shipment of instrument supplies for our students. Strings, shoulder rests, rosin, and everything in between — all carefully sorted and ready to go.

It's moments like these that remind us what Melodia is really about. Not just the performances — but the quiet, behind-the-scenes dedication of our members who show up before the music even starts.

More to come as our team continues to prepare for the season ahead. Stay tuned!
